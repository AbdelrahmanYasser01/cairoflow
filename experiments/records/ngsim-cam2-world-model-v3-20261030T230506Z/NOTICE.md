# Camera 2 tracks checked by camera gate v3 (CC BY-SA 4.0)

`tracks.json` holds SAM 3.1 boxes on NGSIM Lankershim camera 2 footage (source seconds 970-1000), turned into tracks by CairoFlow's procedure, with the observations and the excerpt's manifest; `points.json` holds each measured point; `camera_layer.json` (on go) places the tracks in the world model's frame and lanes. All derive from the camera 2 video, U.S. Department of Transportation Federal Highway Administration. (2016). Next Generation Simulation (NGSIM) Program Lankershim Boulevard Videos. [Dataset]. Provided by ITS DataHub through Data.transportation.gov. Accessed 2026-10-27 from http://doi.org/10.21949/1504477. Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. More: https://ops.fhwa.dot.gov/trafficanalysistools/ngsim.htm The points also derive from the NGSIM trajectories (CC BY-SA 3.0; see the repository NOTICE). Shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, as the overlay (decision D3). Model output and our measurements, not ground truth.
